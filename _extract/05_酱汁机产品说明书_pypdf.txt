Step-by-Step Operation Instructions
6-Step Quick Setup Guide：
Step 1: Silicone Tube Preparation & Connection
a. Locate the pump head and the accompanying silicone tube.
b. Connect one end of the silicone tube firmly to the designated port on the pump head.
c. Check the tube length: If it exceeds the required length for your setup, use a pair of scissors
to trim it to the appropriate size for a neat and unobstructed fit.
Step 2: Loosen the Pump Head Fixing Ring
a. Hold the main body of the pump assembly steady with one hand.
b. Use your other hand to unscrew the fixing ring located at the base of the pump head in a
counterclockwise direction.
c. Loosen the ring sufficiently to prepare for the subsequent lid installation step — do not
remove the ring entirely.
Step 3: Secure the Lid with the Fixing Ring
a. Place the pump head assembly onto the matching lid of the sauce bucket.
b. Align the pump head and lid properly to ensure a seamless fit.
c. Tighten the fixing ring (from Step 2) in a clockwise direction until it is fully secured. This
locks the pump head and lid together firmly, preventing any movement during use.
Step 4: Add Water to the Inner Liner (Water Bath Setup)
a. Take the main unit’s inner liner (stainless steel container) and place it on a stable, flat surface.
b. Pour clean tap water into the liner to a height of 1 inch (2.54 cm).
c. Ensure the water level is precise—this water bath system ensures even and safe heating of
the sauce; do not overfill or underfill the liner.
Step 5: Fill the Sauce Bucket
a. Take the sauce bucket with the assembled pump head and lid (from Step 3).
b. Pour your desired sauce (e.g., nacho cheese, ketchup, hot fudge) into the bucket slowly to
avoid splashing.
c. Fill the bucket to the recommended capacity (do not overfill) to ensure smooth dispensing
and prevent sauce from entering the pump mechanism.
Step 6: Final Assembly & Power On
a. Carefully place the filled sauce bucket (with pump assembly) into the main unit’s inner liner
(with water from Step 4).
b. Ensure the bucket sits securely and centered in the liner without tilting.
c. Connect the power cord of the main unit to a grounded, compatible power outlet (matching
the rated voltage).
d. Turn on the power switch of the machine to activate the warming function and start using
your sauce dispenser.

1.Preparation
a. Place the sauce warmer on a flat, stable, heat-resistant countertop, keep all combustibles
away from the unit at all times.
b. Ensure the power switch/knob is set to the OFF position before connecting to a power
source.
c. Insert your sauce bottle into the warmer’s well first. Fill the well with clean water up to 0.5
inch below the rim—do not overfill or underfill.
2. Heating Operation
a. Connect the unit to a compatible power outlet matching the rated voltage.
b. Adjust the temperature control knob to your desired heating temperature (35° 60° 85°）
c. Allow the sauce and bottle to pre-heat fully before using the pump insert—the pump will not
function properly if contents are not pre-heated.
d. Check the water level frequently during operation; add more water as needed to maintain the
recommended level.
3. Post-Use Shutdown
a. Turn the temperature control knob to the OFF position and disconnect the power plug from
the outlet.
b. Let the unit cool completely before cleaning, refilling, or storing.
c. Remove the sauce bottle and drain any remaining water from the well after cooling.
Pump Disassembly, Cleaning & Reassembly
Clean the pump thoroughly between each use to prevent residue buildup and ensure proper
function.
Disassembly
a. Remove the nut-cylinder and pull the pump-plunger out of the pump chamber.
b. Take the knob off the plunger and disassemble the plunger components.
c. Remove the cap-tube, nut-sleeve-tube, and the outer cover of the pump.
Cleaning-steps
a. Wash all disassembled pump parts with mild soap and warm water.
b. Rinse thoroughly and dry completely before reassembly.
Reassembly
Reverse the disassembly steps to put all pump parts back together, ensuring all components are
properly seated and secured.

General Cleaning & Care
Heating Unit
a. Clean and dry the heating unit after each use and once it has cooled completely.
b. For the exterior: Wipe with a soft cloth and mild soap; do not use metal scouring pads, strong
abrasives, or harsh detergents (may scratch the surface).
c. For the interior (warming well): Metal scouring pads, abrasives, or strong detergents may be
used if necessary for tough residue; rinse and dry thoroughly after cleaning.
Storage
a. Ensure the unit is completely cool, clean, and dry before storage.
b. Set the temperature knob to the OFF position and store in a dry, cool, dust-free area.
c. Keep all parts (pump, unit) together to avoid loss.
Special considerations:
INDUSTRIAL/COMMERCIAL USE ONLY： Not for residential household use. Only operated by
trained personnel.
INDOOR USE ONLY ：Do not use the unit outdoors or in damp/wet environments.
NO WATER = NO OPERATION ：Never run the warmer without water in the well; this causes
uneven heating and damage to the heating element.
DO NOT SUBMERGE ： Never submerge the heating element or any electrical components
(plug, cord, control panel) in water or any liquid to avoid electric shock and equipment failure.
HOT SURFACES ： The pump spout and the entire unit become hot during operation. Always
wear heat-resistant protective gloves when handling the hot unit,pump, or sauce bottle to prevent
severe burns.
WATER LEVEL CONTROL ： Overfilling causes water to overflow when the sauce bottle is
inserted; underfilling results in poor heating performance. Adhere to the ½ inch below rim fill
line.
Combustible Safety ：Maintain a clear distance from all flammable materials, liquids, and gases
at all times during use and cooling.
Electrical Safety ： Do not use the unit with a damaged power cord or plug. If the cord/plug is
damaged, contact the manufacturer for replacement—do not attempt to repair it yourself.
Unattended Use ： Do not leave the unit unattended for extended periods during operation;
check the unit and water level regularly.
